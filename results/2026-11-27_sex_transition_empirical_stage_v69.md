## New descriptive calculation
Daphne jezoensis: existing source-pinned v64 cohorts (Shibata & Kudo 2023, DOI 10.1002/ajb2.16239) allow exact decomposition of fruit per genet F/H = flower per genet F/H × fruit per flower F/H. Both years from ONE study, not two independent species. 2018: 0.2165 × 37.037 = 8.019. 2020: 0.3481 × 26.200 = 9.120. Females have fewer flowers per flowering genet but much higher fruit conversion; observed female advantage survives lower flower output. This arithmetic is not a causal mediation model, no inference on viable adult recruitment.
Mahadura et al. 2026, DOI 10.1002/ece3.74093 Fig 4 observed floret and achene viability means: C. heterophyllum F/H 323/296 and 21.30/6.99; C. palustre 14100/12850 and 86.5/85.7. Ratios 1.091 and 3.047; 1.097 and 1.009. THESE ARE separate marginal summaries, not exact product decomposition of mean viable achenes; reported population mean F/H output 3.77 and 1.26. Direct comparison of fruit/flower vs viability is descriptive, not common-effect pooling. Original authors already identify life-history-specific dominant components. Both species lack significant within-species population relationship between female frequency and female advantage (reported p .391, .443). Contrasts show why pooled between-species relationship is not evidence of a within-population mechanism. New contribution here: stage-ratio accounting for Daphne and comparison of signs, no pooled significance or causal estimate.

## Primary empirical hypothesis (exploratory; hold out future data)
Within a study and comparable endpoints, log(F/H total female output) = log(F/H display) + log(F/H conversion) where denominators match. Define opposed signs (display RR<1, conversion RR>1) vs aligned benefits, quantify covariance at genet level or matched cohort. Compare predictive value of stage contrast and total output in independent population datasets conditional on known sex genetics. Do not use stage components to predict total if definition is arithmetic identity. Independent endpoint for prediction = sex ratio or functional fertility/morphology, not total output.
