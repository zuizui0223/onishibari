# v0.68 | Stage-resolved sexual specialization: meta/macro/simulation integration

**Status:** exploratory source-summary counterfactual and deterministic two-locus recursion, not a new field experiment, an estimated selection coefficient, a meta-analysis, or a phylogenetic result. This branch extends v67 without changing main or PR #3.

## New calculation
All 28 point ratios in Dufay & Billard (2012), Table 6, DOI https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcr062 were transcribed (6 exceed 2); retain endpoint codes and reported nuclear (N)/cytonuclear (NC)/unknown. Direct measures and derived proxies are NOT pooled as one fitness effect. Add the two species summaries from Mahadura et al. (2026), DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.74093, and two within-population years of Daphne from v64/v67 (255/63 vs 53/105 and 205/57 vs 28/71). The Daphne figures are fruits per flowering genet, not lifetime fitness, and the female share is not census sex ratio. Four anchor rows are not four independent species.

A=F/H realized viable maternal recruitment (counterfactually equate reported proxies with A only for sensitivity); t=fraction of viable H maternal recruits produced by selfing. Assume dominant nuclear male-sterility A-locus (Aa females, aa pollen producers), independent dominant b pistil-loss modifier expressed only in aa plants, discrete nonoverlapping generations, constant maternal recruitment, random outcrossing and no paternal pollen limitation or transmission distortion. b carriers in H have zero maternal recruitment, 1+g paternal pollen weight; females unaffected. Resident equilibrium q=max(0,(A-2)/(2(A-1))) for A>2, else q=0. With H frequency h=1-q, total recruitment T=q*A+h and outcrossed O=T-h*t, rare modifier growth crosses one at g*=2*h*T/O-1. This follows from the 2x2 rare-heterozygote matrix: F carrier produces A/(4T) F and H carriers; H carrier produces (1+g)*q*A/(4hT) F and (1+g)*(O-q*A/2)/(2hT) H carriers. For q=0 use the H-only submatrix; at A=2 the female-free equilibrium is nonhyperbolic but the H-only invasion comparison still applies. The modifier is dominant so rare carriers cannot self in H; neutral F carriers outcross. No evolutionary equilibrium or mechanism inferred for the real species. Cost conversion c* uses v67 g=(1+eta*c/(1-c))^beta-1. Under fixed A, increasing t raises g*, and decreasing beta increases c*. The simulation checks 128 A-by-t cases: resident equilibrium preserved and allele decreases/increases on opposite sides of analytical threshold after 100 generations, using a rare H heterozygote. No random seed required.

Cirsium palustre (A=1.26, mean female frequency .193) and C. heterophyllum (3.77, .533) cannot be explained by this simple resident model if these achene ratios were exactly relative recruitment and frequencies represented equilibrium. Nuclear/cytonuclear inheritance, stage differences, age structure, sampling and frequency dependence remain unresolved. This is a counterfactual model diagnostic, NOT evidence of the actual inheritance mechanism. Source summaries and model output in data/2026-11-27_sex_function_transition_v68.json; rerun node scripts/sex_function_transition_v68.js.

## Distinct new research estimands (freeze before external data integration)
1. Meta-analysis: at matched population x year x treatment x independent cohort, record F and H means, SD/SE, independent n and units for flower/ovule production, fruit/seed conversion, germination and later recruitment; male and female gain curves from shared individual datasets separately. log response ratios log(mean_F/mean_H) only for positive means, use binomial likelihood for success/counts including zeros, with appropriate denominators. For matched cohorts, log total output ratio = sum stage log ratios; don't multiply marginal averages from different samples. Propagate within-study covariance or predeclare covariance sensitivity. Treat shared controls, years and species as nested/correlated; phylogenetic covariance plus study random effects. Distinguish natural/hand outcross/self. Primary contrast: does conditional stage of female advantage predict held-out realized sex function better than quantity alone? Morphological pistil retained/lost and actual maternal capacity independently coded. Unknown != absent. Independent external studies held out by genus, do not count v01-v67 as independent.
2. Macro: join Dufay table, original population sex counts, new seed-quality meta data and pollen/fertility gain data by accepted taxon + site/year/study; use species-level joins for priors or coarse comparisons only, not same-population mechanisms. Female count/total beta-binomial where genuine counts exist, separate reported proportions without denominator. Distinguish gynodioecy, cryptic dioecy and morphological dioecy; use independent phylogeny with unknown states. Compare climate-only, genetics/life history, and stage/sex-gain predictors by leave-clade-out; test if male conversion has out-of-clade added predictive skill. Do not use the coarse three-state global dataset (10.5061/dryad.s1rn8pk7n) as gynodioecy labels because it includes gynodioecy in dioecy. Do not classify unknown reproductive function from morphology. No causal transition-rate or ancestral-state claims without enough independent contrasts.
3. Simulation: extend exact resident/invasion model to CMS/restorer, explicit pollen limitation and autonomous/visitor-mediated selfing, overlap generations and resource allocation. Compare genotype-specific predictions; infer no sex mechanism from female frequency alone. Use identifiable combinations and posterior predictive held-out tests rather than fitting all free parameters to q. Temporal variation of female advantage and conversion is an independent next prediction; use observed multiyear sources, not invented environments as data. Future measurement targets: actual saved construction cost c, reallocation eta, experimental paternal elasticity beta, stage-specific viable selfed fraction t, local mate availability, inherited sex determination.
