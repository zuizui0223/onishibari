# v73 | Cytonuclear sex determination and invasion of residual-pistil loss
Model comparison, not a fit to Cirsium or Daphne. New target is the invasion of an independent nuclear pistil-loss modifier conditional on that genetic background, rather than claiming CMS sex ratio theory is new.

Independent dominant loss modifier in H removes its maternal contribution, increases pollen weight by 1+g; in F no phenotype. Evaluate 4000 generations, classify final 300 by stated thresholds (not proof of global equilibrium); only numerically stationary interior states used for invasion threshold, do not interpret boundary or variable cases as stable polymorphisms.
