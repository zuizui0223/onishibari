# v72: life history enters the sex-function transition through differential survival

New exact two-locus demographic recursion extends v68. Adults survive each census with probability s_F, s_H or s_M (pistil-loss modifier in H background); density-regulated offspring fill adult vacancies. All offspring enter immediately; no juvenile lag, overlapping genet/ramet distinctions, age-specific fertility, CMS/restorer, or pollen limitation. A is female/H *viable adult recruits per reproductive census*, not simply achene count; t is fraction of viable H recruits selfed. Nuclear dominant male sterility, independent dominant pistil-loss modifier. Let d_i=1-s_i.

Resident equilibrium: q=0 if A d_H <= 2 d_F, otherwise q=(A d_H - 2 d_F)/(A(d_F+d_H)-2 d_F). At equal survival all q values are exactly v68. Expected adult census duration ratio of females to H is r=d_H/d_F; q formula equivalent to q=(Ar-2)/(A(1+r)-2). For q>0, D=q d_F+(1-q)d_H (vacancies), h=1-q, T=qA+h and O=T-h*t. Modifier invasion threshold 1+g*=2hT d_M/(D O), derived from the rare-carrier survival+recruitment matrix; at q=0 use H-only expression. With equal survival, threshold exactly v68. Fixed relative mortality reduction of M reduces the necessary paternal gain. This is **conditional deterministic selection**, not an estimated selection coefficient. No claim that stage labels alone change the invasion dynamics if A, t, survival and paternal return are the same.

405 parameter-grid cases passed resident-equilibrium, eigenvalue=1 at threshold, and 100-generation directional recurrence checks above/below threshold. 45 equal-survival cases reproduce v68 exactly. No external packages; run node scripts/sex_transition_lifehistory_v72.js.

Cirsium counterfactual diagnostic: if reported population mean viable-achene ratios equal adult recruitment A and reported mean sex ratios represent equilibrium, C. palustre A=1.26 q=.193 requires r=1.826; C. heterophyllum A=3.77 q=.533 requires r=1.672. Neither ratio is measured adult survival. Monocarpic C. palustre cannot acquire this differential via postreproductive adult survival (both reproductive adults die); differences in juvenile recruitment, frequency dependence, inheritance, nonequilibrium remain candidates. Clonal C. heterophyllum requires genet-level demography, not shoot longevity. The original article reports early evidence for cytonuclear sex determination in Cirsium, so the nuclear model is an explicit competing counterfactual rather than an inferred mechanism.

**Theoretical implication:** polycarpy alone is not sufficient for a shift in equilibrium or for a different invasion threshold. Sex-specific longevity, fertility schedule, mating and cost-to-paternity conversion must differ. Stronger test: fit observed cohort-specific survival/recruitment and inheritance rather than add life history as a free post hoc explanation. Next test CMS/restorer and adult survival alternatives with known transmission from primary crossing studies. Independent prediction targets: sex-specific recruitment-to-first-reproduction and genet survival, not same-data q calibration.
