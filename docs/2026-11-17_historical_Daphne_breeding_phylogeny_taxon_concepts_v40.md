## Newly recovered first-party original research sources

### 2011 Japanese Daphne multispecies research results, JSPS 20380093

https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/file/KAKENHI-PROJECT-20380093/20380093seika.pdf

The official 2008–2010 Ryukoku/FFPRI project report explicitly concluded that Japanese ナニワズ (D. jezoensis), オニシバリ (D. pseudomezereum), コショウノキ (D. kiusiana), カラスシキミ (D. miyabeana) were gynodioecious rather than true morphologically strict dioecious plants, with *lower* self-compatibility reported for wintergreen J/P than for evergreen I/M. **No treatment-level hand-cross/self replicates, genet identities, viable seed counts, or data accessions are tabulated.** This is historical direct project-level evidence for focal sexual function, previously incorrectly treated as absent; however it is not usable as a quantitative genotype-level SI estimate or proof of absolute self-incompatibility. Publication list includes a 2010 FFPRI unreviewed research note and conference presentations; no open multilocus nuclear sequence dataset is linked.

### 2020 physiologic paper explicitly cites unpublished P/J one-species phylogeny

Lei, Yamashita, Watanabe, Kawahara & Miyaura, *Physiologia Plantarum* 168:77–87 (2020), DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/ppl.12972.

Authors describe *D. pseudomezereum* and *D. jezoensis* as southern/northern races of one species, attributed to a **T. Kawahara unpublished molecular phylogeny**. The paper's authorship credits T.K. with chloroplast and nuclear phylogenetic analysis, but data/alignments and linked specimen crosswalk are not deposited with the main study or supplements. **This is not reproduced or accepted here as a multilocus taxon phylogeny.** It is a material competing historical taxonomic concept relevant to analyses which assume P/J always independent species.

The same paper's Table 1 labels two summer-green montane study populations in **Nara and Tokushima** as *D. koreana Nakai*. This is NOT sufficient evidence that those Japanese plants equal the Korean lineage renamed *D. kamtschatica var. koreana* after Han et al. 2025. Treat the Japanese study populations as a **distinct unresolved Honshu/Shikoku OTU** pending new vouchers and genetic genotyping. The same nominal epithet is not identity proof.

### A functional selfing contrast not captured by binary sex labels

Sakata & Nakahama (2018), *Plant Species Biology* DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/1442-1984.12212, studied *D. miyabeana*: open-canopy site natural fruit set 31.22±3.07% (mean±SE), closed-canopy site 9.01±1.71%; spontaneous selfing is possible but less effective than manual outcrossing; bees visit in open canopy, largely nonpollinating ants in shade. This is independent experimental evidence for pollen route plasticity, not evidence that P or J can automatically self. Together with 2011 FFPRI annual report's *D. kiusiana* H vs F successful hand-cross, it exposes multiple **functional routes** under similar morphological sex labels.

### Contrasting literature in 2020/2025

- Nitta & Uchida (2020), J Jpn Bot 95:341–347 Table1 p.343: *D. pseudomezereum var. pseudomezereum* dioecy (Yonekura 2017 cited), *D. jezoensis* gynodioecy, *D. kamtschatica* hermaphrodite, old var. koreana sex unknown. Morphology/status claim versus 2011 KAKEN functional report; not a same-genet reciprocal crossing.
- Han et al. (2025), DOI https://doi.org/10.11110/kjpt.2025.55.3.157: Korean C plastome sisters K, nuclear nrITS sisters P. The source markers were reproduced previously in this branch; ITS PC from 6–8 quartet-informative positions, chloroplast CK from 26 informative positions after removing duplicate IR. Organellar linkage and nrITS concerted evolution mean mechanism (ILS/introgression) remains unidentifiable.
- Bonnaudet et al. (2026), family Angiosperms353 DOI https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcag206: 349 independent-source gene trees with 162 four-way proxy *Daphne* quartets, **none of P/C/K**, therefore cannot resolve this historical focal claim.

## Mandatory sampling/analysis gates

1. Use voucher- and region-specific taxon concept IDs. Japanese *D. koreana* from Lei's Nara/Tokushima sites, historical *D. pseudomezereum var. koreana* from Honshu, and Korean *D. kamtschatica var. koreana* are **not automatically synonymized as one operational genetic lineage**.
2. Distinguish the historical name *D. kamtchatica var. jezoensis* (modern *D. jezoensis*) from *D. kamtschatica var. kamtschatica*. This alias mistake would corrupt ancestral sex states and selfing-coding.
3. Cross only verified functional phenotypes (hand self, hand cross, bag control, viable seed, actual male paternity), not 3 printed sex-system words. KAKEN "low self-compatibility" is a **reported qualitative prior**, not proof of physiological self incompatibility in focal P.
4. For introgression vs ILS, obtain **matched same-genet organelle and 100+ independent low-copy nuclear loci across P/C/K**; no conclusion from historical unpublished chloroplast/nrITS alone or nonfocal proxy loci.
5. Model leaf phenology, canopy, and pollination guild as competing environment mediators and confounders, not evidence that wintergreen evolved *because* gynodioecy advanced. The 2011 phylogeny assertion is not a recovered topology.
