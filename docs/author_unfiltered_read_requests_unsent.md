## A. For 2023 Korean *Daphne* plastome study
To: Sang-Hun Oh <soh42@dju.kr>
Subject: Request for unfiltered total-DNA reads underlying Daphne var. koreana plastome ON244034

Dear Prof. Oh,

I am conducting a comparative study of *Daphne pseudomezereum*, *D. kamtschatica*, and the Korean taxon formerly known as *D. pseudomezereum* var. *koreana*, with particular interest in whether their plastid–nuclear ITS discordance reflects incomplete lineage sorting or historical gene flow.

In Yoo, Park & Oh (2023; DOI: 10.1080/23802359.2023.2179356), you reported 7.34 Gbp of raw Illumina reads for voucher Lee 7891 (Gangneung; plastome ON244034). The currently accessible SRR19117239 consists of 359,842,060 bases. In a preliminary quality check, virtually all publicly accessible reads mapped to the plastome, with no detectable coverage of the nuclear ITS region.

Would the **original unfiltered total-DNA FASTQ files** (before any organellar read selection) still be archived, or have they been deposited under another accession? If they are available and suitable for scholarly reuse, I would be grateful to discuss access and appropriate attribution/collaboration. The purpose is to test whether independent low-copy nuclear loci and ITS variants can be recovered from the *same voucher* as ON244034.

I would also appreciate any available library preparation or sample-sheet metadata that could clarify whether SRR19117239 was intended as an organelle-enriched submission.

Thank you for considering this request.

## B. For 2025 Korean taxonomic reassessment
To: Jung-Hyun Lee <quercus@jnu.ac.kr>
Subject: Inquiry about original Illumina reads for Daphne pseudomezereum / kamtschatica (2025)

Dear Prof. Lee,

I have been reading Han et al. (2025; DOI: 10.11110/kjpt.2025.55.3.157) and am investigating its interesting discordance between the plastome (Korean var. *koreana* + *D. kamtschatica*) and nrITS (Korean var. *koreana* + Japanese *D. pseudomezereum*) phylogenies. Our reanalysis supports that the conflict is robust at the marker level, but does not distinguish ancient introgression from incomplete lineage sorting.

Could you please advise whether the **original, unfiltered Illumina total-DNA FASTQ files** underlying plastomes PV752465 (Japanese *D. pseudomezereum*, voucher Lee-Dpp230303) and PV752464 (*D. kamtschatica*, voucher K005971094) are publicly available under any BioProject/SRA/DRA accession, or might be available for an agreed research use? I would also be interested to know whether comparable nuclear reads exist for the Korean voucher Byeon-Dpk250701, from which ITS PX056848 was obtained.

If suitable data are available, our intended first step is a limited pilot to recover independent low-copy nuclear loci and to evaluate gene-tree concordance, with full attention to accession provenance, credit, and potential collaboration. We have not reached any conclusion about a specific hybridization mechanism.

Thank you very much for your time.
